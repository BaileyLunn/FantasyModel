# Recency bias: sample weights + recent-form EWMA (built 2026-09-25)

Goal: a noticeable but not huge bias toward recent performance, without giving up accuracy.

## What changed

1. **Recency sample weights** (`fantasy_model/weights.py`, `training.recency_half_life_seasons` in `configs/default.yaml`).
   `w = 0.5 ** (age / half_life)`, with age in seasons measured from the newest training row
   (`season + (week-1)/18`, so weeks within a season decay too). Weights are re-referenced to the newest row of each fit
   (validation fit and production refit) and normalized to mean 1. **Chosen: 4 seasons.** Production refit (through 2026 wk2),
   mean weight per season relative to 2026: 2025 0.91, 2024 0.76, 2023 0.64, 2022 0.54, 2020 0.38, 2016 0.19.
2. **Recent-form EWMA features** (`features.recent_form_enabled`, `features.ewm_halflife_games`): `fp_ewm`, `targets_ewm`,
   `carries_ewm`, `rec_ewm` (pipeline, `shift(1)` then `ewm(halflife, ignore_na=True)` inside each player's history; zero-stat
   rows count as 0 usage) and `snap_pct_ewm` (panel build, as-of join on strictly earlier weeks). They cross season boundaries
   like `fp_roll`. **Chosen: half-life 5 games** (last game 1.0, 5 games ago 0.5, 10 ago 0.25). The old 3-game rolling means stay.
3. `update_week.py` uses both by default for every profile (half + PPR); `--recency-half-life` / `--ewm-halflife` override (0 = off).
   `scripts/tune_recency.py` reruns the grid. `project.py` exposes `projection_features()` (used by the backtest) and the
   projection CSVs gained `fp_roll`, `fp_ewm`, `snap_pct_ewm` columns.

## Why these values

* EWMA features are the part that helps: at every weight setting, adding them improves the 2025 holdout RMSE/R², and 5 games is as good as or
  better than 2–3 games on RMSE/R² for 2026 and the roster backtest (single seed and 5-seed mean).
  8 games scored best on 2026 but is the edge of the grid and barely "recent" (≈ a season-long average), so it was not picked.
* Sample weights are roughly accuracy-neutral at 4–6 seasons and cost a little at ≤3 (1 season is clearly worse:
  PPR 2026 R² 0.299 without EWMA). Averaged over 5 seeds, 4 vs unweighted (with EWMA 5): 2025 MAE +0.00, 2026 RMSE +0.025,
  roster MAE −0.012 / RMSE +0.018 — within seed noise (sd ≈ 0.01). 4 is the most recent-leaning value in the requested 2–4 range
  that does not lose accuracy; 6 is equally good but a weaker bias.

## Week 3 projections

Production models refit on all 83,685 labelled rows (2016–2025 + 2026 wk1–2) with the chosen settings →
`reports/projections_2026_w3_{ppr,half}.csv` (496 ACT QB/RB/WR/TE each; the weekly roster was re-fetched, previously 499).
Median change vs the previous projections is ~0; largest moves come from the EWMA smoothing of short/injury-shortened
3-game windows (e.g. Puka Nacua 8.2 → 13.3 PPR, Drew Lock 13.4 → 9.1).

## Caveats

* 2026 wk1–2 slices are small (626 / 904 rows); differences of a few hundredths are noise.
* The weighting is relative to the newest training row, so early-season refits give the new season little data but full weight.
